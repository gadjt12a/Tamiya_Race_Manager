TAMIYA RACE MANAGER - PLEASE READ BEFORE INSTALLING
====================================================

YOUR RACE DATA IS SAFE
----------------------
Your race database (seasons, racers, points) is stored SEPARATELY
from the app, in:

    %LOCALAPPDATA%\TamiyaRaceManager\

This installer only replaces the app itself. It does not read,
modify or delete your race data - not on install, not on update,
not on uninstall.

UPDATING FROM AN OLDER (ZIP-STYLE) VERSION?
-------------------------------------------
Your old race data is NOT deleted - it stays in the old
race-manager folder. To bring it into the new app (one time only):

  1. Launch the new version (desktop icon).
  2. Click "Import Data" (top right of the home screen).
  3. Choose the file  data\racedata.json  inside your OLD
     race-manager folder - or your latest "Export Data" file.
  4. Seasons, racers and points all come across.

If your old version ran WITHOUT Python (it warned about "browser
storage mode"), there is no data file on disk - open the OLD version
first, click "Export Data", then import that file as above.

GOOD PRACTICE
-------------
Even though updates are safe, we recommend clicking "Export Data"
and saving to a USB stick before updating - and after every race
night. Automatic daily backups are also kept in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\TamiyaRaceManager\backups\ (last 14 days).
